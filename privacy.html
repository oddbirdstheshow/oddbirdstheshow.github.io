Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Who uses Oddbirds Studio

Oddbirds Studio is used only by the Oddbirds team, with the Oddbirds team's own TikTok account. It does not collect data from members of the public.

What data it accesses

When the account owner connects TikTok, the tool receives basic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar) and permission to upload videos to that account as drafts. It does not read messages, followers or any other account data.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the account owner's own computer and are used solely to upload Oddbirds videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

The account owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. Revoking access stops the tool from uploading.

The Oddbirds waitlist

If you join the waitlist on our website, we collect your email address and the products you said you'd want. We use them only to tell you when preorders open and to decide which products to make first. They're stored with Kit, the email service we use to send those messages. We don't sell them, share them with anyone else, or use them for advertising. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your details at any time by writing to the address below.
