Five of the birds, one feeling each. Save them to your phone and turn them into stickers in a few taps, on iPhone and Android. How to do it is just below.

Download all five (zip)

iPhone iOS 17 or newer

  1. Tap Save under a sticker, then Save Image. It goes to your Photos.
  2. Open it in Photos and press and hold on the bird until it lifts off the white.
  3. Tap Add Sticker. It's now in your stickers in Messages, WhatsApp and the keyboard, on all your Apple devices.

To send one: in Messages, tap +, then Stickers.

Android with WhatsApp, on any Android phone

  1. Tap Save under a sticker. It goes to your Downloads or Gallery.
  2. In WhatsApp, open any chat, tap the sticker icon in the message box, then Create.
  3. Pick the bird you saved. WhatsApp cuts it out for you. Tap Send and it stays in your stickers.

Samsung Galaxy: open it in Gallery, press and hold the bird, tap Save as sticker. It shows up in Samsung Keyboard's stickers.
