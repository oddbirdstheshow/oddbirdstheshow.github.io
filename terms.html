What Oddbirds Studio is

Oddbirds Studio is an internal tool used by the Oddbirds team to upload our own original animated videos to our own TikTok account as drafts. It is not a public service and does not accept new users.

Use of TikTok

Oddbirds Studio uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Videos are sent to the account owner's TikTok inbox and are only published if the account owner chooses to post them in the TikTok app.

All videos uploaded through Oddbirds Studio are original Oddbirds content owned by the Oddbirds team.
